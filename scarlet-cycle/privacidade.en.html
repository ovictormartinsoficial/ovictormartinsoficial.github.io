Privacy policy

This policy describes Scarlet Cycle for Android, developed by Victor Martins. The app is being prepared for publication. This page will be reviewed to reflect the final advertising settings and distribution countries.

Personal records on your device

The name, date of birth, period records, symptoms, body information, and fertility or pregnancy records you provide are stored on your device. The diary is encrypted with AES-GCM and a key protected by Android Keystore. We do not operate a server to receive these records or require a Scarlet online account.

How we use information

The app uses your records locally to organize your diary, display charts, calculate estimates and track your chosen goals. Your date of birth is used to enforce the minimum age of 13. Health data and your name are not sent to AdMob as advertising parameters.

Advertising and technical data

The ad-supported version uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). Even with test or non-personalized ads, the SDK may process your IP address, interactions with ads and the app, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers, depending on the applicable settings. Google uses this data for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention; it is not your menstrual diary data. Processing may involve servers outside your country.

See Google's privacy policy and the SDK data documentation. Scarlet's records and calculations work offline; ads require a connection.

Permissions and reminders

Scarlet requests notification permission for local reminders. You can turn them off or change their time in the app, and manage sound and permissions in Android settings. Lock-screen content visibility depends on your device settings.

PDFs and backups you choose to create

When you export a PDF report, the file contains the information selected by the app for your appointment, including your registered name. The PDF does not have the diary's encryption: protect it and share it only with people you choose. Backups exported by the app are protected by the password you set. The location you choose for saving or sharing files may belong to another provider and is subject to that provider's policies.

Retention and deletion

Records remain on your device until you delete them. You can use the app's deletion controls or clear Scarlet's data in Android settings. Uninstalling removes local private data; PDFs, backups and shared copies must be deleted separately. We do not hold a server copy of your diary to recover or delete remotely. Retention periods for technical data processed by Google follow Google's policies.

Teenagers

Registration requires an age of at least 13. This does not replace local requirements for consent and the protection of teenagers. Advertising and consent settings must respect users' ages, supported countries and store policies.

This website

This website has no health forms, advertising or visitor analytics tools of its own. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process technical access information to operate and protect the hosting service, under its privacy policy.

Contact and rights

For support, privacy questions and requests about your data, email ktcorpofficial@gmail.com. Do not send health information in public comments or repositories. You may request explanations about data processing and exercise the rights applicable in your region; local controls let you access, export and delete your records on your device.
