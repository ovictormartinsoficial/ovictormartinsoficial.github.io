Your records

To export or delete data, use the app's privacy and backup controls. Keep your backup password safe: we cannot access it and do not hold a server copy of your diary.

Reminders

Check Scarlet's notification permissions, your notification volume and Android's Do Not Disturb mode. Battery settings may delay reminders.

Privacy when asking for help

Do not post your diary, reports, date of birth or other health information in a public repository. When asking for help, describe only the technical issue that needs attention.
